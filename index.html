Frequently Asked Questions

How do payments work? Do I need a credit card deposit?

Zero deposit is required to reserve! You pay our team directly when you arrive in Luxor. We accept cash in US Dollars ($), Euros (€), British Pounds (£), or Egyptian Pounds (EGP), as well as major international credit cards.

What if I haven't booked a hotel in Luxor yet?

Our team arranges everything! We partner with verified 5-star Nile view resorts, peaceful West Bank heritage villas with swimming pools, and traditional Dahabiya cruise boats. Simply select "I need hotel accommodation" on the booking form.

What happens if the Hot Air Balloon is grounded due to wind?

Safety is always #1. If Egyptian Civil Aviation cancels morning flights due to high winds, you are never charged a penny! We will either reschedule your flight for the following morning or proceed with your temple tours.

Are temple tours private or in shared large buses?

All our land temple expeditions are 100% private! You will have your own modern air-conditioned vehicle, private driver, and a licensed private Egyptologist guide. You set the pace—stay as long as you wish at each monument.

Is Luxor safe for families and solo female travelers?

Yes, absolutely! Luxor is one of the friendliest and safest travel destinations in Egypt, with welcoming locals and dedicated tourism police everywhere. With Luxor Booking Tours as your trusted local partner and trusted local guardian, you are escorted comfortably door-to-door from your hotel or cruise ship throughout your journey.

Can I design a custom private itinerary (Tailor-Made Route)?

Every journey is 100% flexible! If you want to combine Luxor with Aswan, sail on an intimate Dahabiya, visit the colored astronomical ceilings of Dendera, or add extra tombs like King Tutankhamun and Seti I, simply message our team on WhatsApp and we will build your personalized dream itinerary within minutes.
